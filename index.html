Founding Doctrine
"Someone has to say the quiet part on a nicely designed website, and Brookings wasn't returning our calls."

The League was founded on the belief that sound strategic analysis shouldn't require a board of directors, an endowment, or peer review — just two people with strong opinions, a shared Google Doc, and a willingness to publish on the internet before anyone can stop them.

We are independent in the fullest sense: independently funded (by our own savings, dwindling), independently staffed (by us), and independently reviewed (also by us).

The Full Roster

Two scholars. No support staff. No exceptions.

JK
Jackson Karas
Co-Founder & Director of Analysis

Jackson previously worked at the Institute for the Study of War, where he tracked conflicts most people couldn't find on a map, for an audience mostly made up of other people who study war. He now applies that same granular, source-heavy rigor to the League's core output: strongly worded assessments that no government agency asked for and no government agency reads.

He believes every crisis can be explained with the right map, the right timeline, and enough confidence in his own voice.

Ex-ISW Map Enthusiast Cites Primary Sources
ST
Sebastian Teuscher
Co-Founder & Chief Economic Officer

Sebastian passed the CFA Level I exam and then, after absorbing what colleagues describe as "approximately 1,000" job rejections with remarkable composure, concluded that the finance industry simply wasn't ready for his blend of discounted cash flow modeling and unsolicited geopolitical commentary.

He now serves as the League's self-appointed authority on sanctions regimes, the bond market's feelings, and why "the yield curve is basically screaming at us."
