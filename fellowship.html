Visiting Fellowship Program

Become a Senior Non-Resident Fellow. Today.

The League's fellowship is unpaid, unofficial and entirely ceremonial. It does, however, come with a title long enough to fill an entire LinkedIn headline, which is the real currency of the think tank world.

What You Get
  • The title "Senior Non-Resident Fellow." Non-resident because we don't have a building.
  • Permission to say "at the League, we think…" at dinner parties.
  • Read access to the group chat's pinned messages. There is one pinned message. It is a map.
  • One (1) annual performance review, conducted by yourself.
  • Eligibility for the League's distinguished honor, the "Called It" Award, awarded retroactively to whoever called it.
What You Don't Get
  • Money. Any amount of it. To be fair us, we don't get any either.
  • An office, desk, badge, lanyard or parking space.
  • A vote on the board. The board is two people and they like it that way.
  • Access to the actual group chat. Some things are classified.
  • Any guarantee of being taken seriously. We've checked, and we can't offer that to ourselves either.

Frequently Asked

Questions We Have Been Asked (Once)

Is this a real fellowship?

It is exactly as real as the League. Interpret that however you like.

Can I put it on my résumé?

We can't stop you. One of our co-founders has submitted roughly 1,000 résumés and can confirm that nobody checks.

Is there an application deadline?

Applications are accepted on a rolling basis, which is a professional way of saying we haven't thought about it.

What happens after I'm accepted?

Nothing. That's the beauty of it.

Can I become a co-founder instead?

The League will accept a third co-founder with a strong LinkedIn banner. Board approval required (estimated deliberation time: four minutes).
